### Welcome!
My research interests are at the intersection of privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) and artificial intelligence (AI), with a focus on developing practical privacy-preserving systems for AI and data science.
Specifically, I aim to enable the deployment of systems with meaningful privacy guarantees through strategic algorithmic design that improves privacy trade-offs and rigorous auditing that uncovers privacy oversights.
My research spans the fields of differential privacy, secure computation, and evolutionary algorithms.
To learn more about my research, check out my [publications page](/publications)!

I am currently a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science at the University of Sydney. Previously, I completed my PhD and Master's degree at the University of Waterloo, where I worked with [Florian Kerschbaum](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~fkerschb) in the [Cryptography, Security, and Privacy (CrySP) Lab](https://crysp.uwaterloo.ca). I have also interned at Microsoft Research with the Azure Research Security and Privacy group in Cambridge and the Algorithms Group in Redmond.
